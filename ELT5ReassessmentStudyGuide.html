Cyber 101 - Conditional Statements
IMPORTANT: Read Carefully

Re-assessment is an opportunity for reflection and growth, not simply a chance to "try again." The purpose of re-assessment is to demonstrate that you have invested time in understanding the concepts you initially struggled with and have developed stronger skills through deliberate practice.

This mandatory study guide is designed to help you identify gaps in your understanding and build the knowledge needed for success. Completing this guide shows that you are taking ownership of your learning and are committed to improving your skills.

You must complete and submit this study guide BEFORE attempting your re-assessment. Failure to do so will result in Mrs. Wright not scoring your re-assessment. Skipping this preparation demonstrates that you are not investing in your own growth and are not ready to show improved understanding.

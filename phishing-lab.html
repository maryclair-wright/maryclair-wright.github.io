Mission Brief

What You Are Doing

Write a fake email to your assigned adult on campus. Your job is to make it convincing enough that they actually write back. The more realistic it feels, the better your shot at winning.

Think about what makes people click or respond: a sense of urgency, something exciting, a question they feel like they have to answer. Use what you know.

Requirements

Your email needs to have

  • [+] A believable reason for sending it -- something that makes sense
  • [+] Something that makes the person want to write back
  • [+] A subject line that makes them want to open it
  • [+] Language that is appropriate for school
Hard Limits

Your email cannot

  • [x] Ask for passwords, personal info, or anything private
  • [x] Include anything inappropriate, mean, or embarrassing
  • [x] Be sent to anyone other than your assigned adult
  • [x] Use private things you know about the person to trick or embarrass them
Toolkit

Tricks Phishers Use

These are ideas to try, not a checklist. Pick the ones that fit your email and use them naturally. Need a sketchy link to drop in? this one has never let anyone down.

  • [!]
    Make it feel urgent -- Act like something bad will happen if they do not respond fast. "Your account will be deleted tonight." People stop thinking carefully when they feel panicked.
  • [!]
    Use a weird or generic greeting -- Real phishing emails often say things like "Dear Valued Customer" or get the name slightly wrong. It is a small thing most people ignore.
  • [!]
    Promise something too good -- Tell them they won something, or that they have been selected for something special. Make it sound exciting but just believable enough.
  • [!]
    Mention a random attachment -- Reference a file they did not ask for and would not expect. "See the attached form." "Your document is ready." Most people will be curious.
  • [!]
    Throw in a typo on purpose -- A small spelling mistake can make an email look more human and less suspicious. Just do not overdo it.
  • [!]
    Add a weird link or image -- Drop in a link or picture that does not quite fit -- a URL that looks almost right, or an image that has nothing to do with what you are talking about.

Key Concepts to Remember

For Loops: Use when you know how many times to repeat

While Loops: Use when you repeat until a condition becomes false

Nested Loops: A loop inside another loop - the inner loop completes all iterations for each iteration of the outer loop

Loop Variables: Track their values carefully as they change each iteration

Question 15
